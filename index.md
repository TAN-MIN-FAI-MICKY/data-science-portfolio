## Featured project: Bike Sharing Demand Analysis

### Research question

How do season, weather conditions, and temperature relate to daily bike-sharing demand?

### Findings

This project analyzes daily bike rentals in Washington, D.C. from 2011 to 2012. The data shows a positive association between normalized feeling temperature and rentals (Pearson correlation: **0.63**). Average daily rentals were highest in September 2012 (**7,286**) and lowest in January 2011 (**1,232**). Clear or partly cloudy days had more rentals on average than light rain or snow days.
